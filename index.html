About the portal

Placement work usually runs on notice boards, forms and spreadsheets. This system moves it online: student profiles, company requirements, applications and results all live in one place, so nothing gets lost and no one applies for a role they cannot get.

  1. Build your profile

    Add academics, skills and a PDF resume once.

  2. Find and apply

    Search jobs, see instantly whether you qualify, then apply.

  3. Track the outcome

    Follow status from Applied to Selected and get notified at each step.

Features

Automatic eligibility

CGPA, department, backlogs, skills, batch and percentages are checked against every job, with clear reasons when you fall short.

Search and filters

Filter by company, role, location, salary, department, CGPA and job type without reloading the page.

Application tracking

Colour-coded statuses show where each application stands, and duplicates are blocked.

Notifications

New postings, shortlists, interview schedules and results arrive in your notification page.

Admin dashboard

Manage students, companies, jobs and applications, and see placement charts at a glance.

Secure by design

Hashed passwords, role-based access, CSRF protection and private resume storage.
